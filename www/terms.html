1. Platform Purpose & Electronic Signature Validity

Thinkable Vault provides digital contract drafting, bilateral remote signing, and cryptographic evidentiary certification under Section 10A of the Information Technology Act, 2000. Contracts formed through mutual digital signing on this platform constitute legally binding covenants under Section 10 of the Indian Contract Act, 1872.

2. User Representations & Mutual Debt Acknowledgment

When parties execute a Mutual Loan Promissory Note on the platform, the Borrower acknowledges receipt of the principal amount and covenants to repay according to the agreed schedule. When parties execute a Working Agreement (SOW), the Client covenants to disburse milestone payments upon deliverable approval, and the Contractor assigns all Intellectual Property upon full payment.

3. Non-Lending & Intermediary Disclaimer

Thinkable Vault is not a bank, NBFC, or credit intermediary. We do not underwrite loans, guarantee repayment, or act as an escrow agent. Users are solely responsible for ensuring transactions comply with applicable state money lending regulations and personal income tax reporting.

4. Section 65B Electronic Evidentiary Admissibility

All instruments registered on the platform are sealed with SHA-256 cryptographic hashes and paired with an electronic certificate adhering to Section 65B(4) of the Indian Evidence Act, 1872 / Section 63 of Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023. These certificates are admissible as primary electronic evidence before civil courts and arbitral tribunals across India.

These terms and any electronic instruments generated on the platform are governed by the laws of the Republic of India. Unless specified otherwise in a custom agreement clause, courts situated at the jurisdiction chosen by the drafting party shall have exclusive jurisdiction.
