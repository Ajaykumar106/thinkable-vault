Privacy Policy for Thinkable Vault

Effective Date: November 9, 2026 · Governed by Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 & IT Act, 2000

1. Overview & Custodian Role

Thinkable Vault ("we", "our", or "the Platform") operates as a decentralized digital custodian for bilateral legal contracts, mutual loan promissory notes, and freelance working agreements (SOW). We provide cryptographic timestamping, SHA-256 document hashing, and Section 65B electronic record certificates under Indian Evidence law.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Identifiers: Full legal name, verified email address, chosen username, and system-generated Unique Vault ID (e.g. VID-XXXXX).
  • Optional UPI Virtual Private Address (VPA): If you configure a recipient UPI handle (e.g. name@okhdfcbank or mobile@paytm), it is stored exclusively to generate 1-tap peer-to-peer payment deep links for your counterparties. We never request, handle, or store bank account passwords, UPI MPINs, or payment card details.
  • Agreement Instrument Metadata: Party names, disbursement dates, maturity dates, agreed deliverables, payment milestones, drawn digital signatures, and 12-digit UPI transaction reference (UTR) numbers entered by users.
  • Cryptographic Audit Logs: Timestamps, IP addresses at the time of remote electronic signing, and SHA-256 document hashes generated to certify non-repudiation.

3. How Payment Data & UPI Deep Linking Operates

Thinkable Vault is not a payment aggregator or non-banking financial company (NBFC). All payments occur directly between users using National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI) standard UPI URI deep links launched inside registered banking and UPI apps (such as Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, or BHIM). No funds pass through our bank accounts.

4. Section 65B Statutory Electronic Custody

In accordance with Section 65B of the Indian Evidence Act, 1872 (and Section 63 of the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023), electronic records created on Thinkable Vault are maintained on tamper-evident cloud infrastructure (Neon Serverless PostgreSQL with SSL encryption in transit and AES-256 at rest) to enable automated evidentiary certification in Indian judicial and arbitration proceedings.

5. Data Retention & User Rights

Users may request deletion of their draft agreements at any time. Once both parties have digitally signed and executed a legal agreement, cryptographic digests and transaction references are retained in the vault ledger to protect legal admissibility for both parties.
